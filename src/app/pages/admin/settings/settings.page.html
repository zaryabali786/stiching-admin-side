Customers can ask for priority stitching when they create an order. You decide here how many days it takes; the number is shown to them and becomes the order's due date.

Fast shipping

Customers can ask for fast shipping when they create an order. You decide here how many days the delivery takes; the number is shown to them and to the team on the order. Fast shipping has no price: it is never added to an order or an invoice.

Whole days, 1 to 90. Counted from the day the order is dispatched.
